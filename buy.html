Buying Guide

How to Buy a Home

Follow our step-by-step guide to a smooth and confident home-buying journey.

1

Determine Your Budget

Calculate what you can afford by considering your income, savings, and monthly expenses. Get pre-approved for a mortgage to know your exact price range.

2

Find the Right Neighborhood

Research locations based on commute, schools, amenities, and future value. Use our location filters to narrow down your ideal area.

3

Tour Homes & Shortlist

Schedule viewings with our trusted agents. Take notes, compare properties, and shortlist your favorites. Don't rush — this is a big decision.

4

Make an Offer & Negotiate

Work with your agent to submit a competitive offer. Negotiate price, contingencies, and closing timeline based on market conditions.

5

Close & Get Your Keys

Complete inspections, finalize your mortgage, and sign the paperwork. Once closed, you'll receive the keys to your new home!
