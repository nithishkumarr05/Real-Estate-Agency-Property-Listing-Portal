About Our Agency

A Legacy of Real Estate Excellence

Founded in 2014 in the heart of New York, EstateHub began as a small boutique agency with one simple belief — that buying or selling a home should feel personal, transparent, and empowering. Over a decade later, we've grown into a nationwide network of 150+ agents, yet that founding principle remains unchanged.

We combine local market intelligence with modern technology to deliver a seamless experience — from the first viewing to the final signature. Whether you're a first-time buyer, a seasoned investor, or looking to rent, our team is dedicated to finding the perfect match for your lifestyle and budget.
